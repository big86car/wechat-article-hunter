# Originality Rubric

## Blocking conditions

- unknown or disallowed rights basis for a material source
- unattributed quotation or reproduction of source-specific data/graphics
- close paraphrase of distinctive language or anecdotes
- substantial reuse of one source's title logic, section sequence, examples, and conclusion
- no concrete original contribution beyond summary or wording changes
- instructions or transformations intended to evade platform or plagiarism detection

## Evidence of original value

At least one contribution must be material and demonstrated:

- a defensible thesis not inherited from the reference article
- a new synthesis across independent primary sources
- an original analytical framework or comparison criteria
- authorized first-party observations, data, interviews, or experiments
- a materially different audience problem and application analysis
- a substantive counterargument and conclusion derived from the evidence

## Review dimensions

Score each from 1–5:

| Dimension | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Provenance completeness | material sources unknown | all material sources and permitted uses recorded |
| Attribution integrity | material attribution missing | quotations and source-specific elements correctly attributed |
| Expression independence | distinctive wording closely reused | wording independently composed |
| Structural independence | source structure substantially replicated | argument structure independently designed |
| Original value | only summary/paraphrase | substantial, clearly articulated contribution |

A `pass` requires every dimension ≥4, no blocking finding, and at least one material original contribution.
