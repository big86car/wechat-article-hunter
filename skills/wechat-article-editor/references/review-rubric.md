# Review Rubric

Score 1–5 and justify each dimension:

| Dimension | Blocking condition |
|---|---|
| Evidence integrity | fabricated source, unsupported material claim, or citation mismatch |
| Reasoning | conclusion materially exceeds evidence or central contradiction |
| Structure | thesis cannot be identified or sections do not build toward it |
| Reader value | article lacks a concrete takeaway for the target audience |
| Native style | pervasive translation-shaped or formulaic prose |
| Publication readiness | unresolved placeholders, wrong title/body, missing disclosure, or unsafe claim |

`blocking` prevents finalization. `major` significantly reduces trust or comprehension. `minor` improves polish but does not change the argument.

Do not flatten a distinctive voice into generic corporate prose. Do not “fact-check” an opinion as if it were a fact; check whether its factual premises are sound and whether inference is labeled.
