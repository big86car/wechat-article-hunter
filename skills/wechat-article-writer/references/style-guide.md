# Chinese WeChat Writing Guide

## Prefer

- conclusion-led openings and specific nouns/verbs
- short, logically connected paragraphs
- explicit causal boundaries: “可能意味着”, “现有证据只能说明”
- examples that carry an argument rather than decorate it
- occasional direct questions when they sharpen the reader's decision

## Avoid

- “在当今这个飞速发展的时代” and other empty scene-setting
- repeated “首先、其次、最后” when relationships are not sequential
- exaggerated claims such as “彻底颠覆”, “史诗级”, or “所有人都” without evidence
- fake intimacy, excessive exclamation marks, emoji walls, and motivational filler
- translation-shaped Chinese, noun stacks, and long sentences with many nested clauses
- repeating the same conclusion in the title, opening, every section, and ending
- invented anecdotes or vague authority such as “有研究表明” without a source

## Rhythm

Use a mix of compact paragraphs and occasional structured lists. A list is justified when items are parallel; use prose for causal explanation. Tables are appropriate for exact comparisons, not emotional emphasis.
