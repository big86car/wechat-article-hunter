# Content Acquisition Policy

## Allowed inputs

- globally accessible public pages obtained through ordinary browsing or documented APIs
- files, text, screenshots, exports, and archives provided by the user
- content owned by the user or used under a documented license
- authenticated integrations the user is authorized to operate

## Not part of this skill

- extracting or refreshing private WeChat session keys/tokens
- bypassing CAPTCHA, access controls, rate limits, or IP restrictions
- emulating undocumented client protocols or maintaining device fingerprints
- proxy rotation intended to defeat platform enforcement
- bulk collection where terms, authorization, or reuse rights are unclear

If an article is inaccessible, ask for a user-provided export or use other authoritative sources. Do not downgrade the evidence standard merely because the preferred competitor article cannot be acquired.

## Provenance record

For every supplied or competitor artifact, capture:

- stable URL or local path
- acquisition mode and date
- rights basis: `public`, `user_owned`, `licensed`, or `authorized_export`
- permitted transformation: quote, summarize, analyze, or idea lead only
- source-specific elements that must not be reproduced: distinctive language, anecdotes, sequence, graphics, or proprietary data
