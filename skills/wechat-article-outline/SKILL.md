# WeChat Article Outline

Turn research into one coherent argument. Read the selected topic card, research pack, source ledger, and brief. Do not let the outline become a pile of facts.

## Build the Argument

1. State the audience's concrete tension.
2. Write a one-sentence thesis that is specific enough to challenge.
3. Choose the structure from [references/structure-patterns.md](references/structure-patterns.md) that best fits the content type.
4. Give each section one job in proving or qualifying the thesis.
5. Map evidence IDs to claims and mark inference explicitly.
6. Place the strongest counterargument before the conclusion.
7. Identify only visuals that materially improve understanding.

## Output `outline.md`

Include:

- audience, objective, recommended title, alternative titles
- opening hook and why it earns attention
- thesis in one sentence
- section-by-section plan with purpose, key claim, evidence IDs, example, transition, approximate length, and optional visual
- counterargument and response
- conclusion takeaway and restrained call to action
- total target length and evidence gaps

The recommended title must match the article's real claim. Avoid premature certainty, manufactured suspense, and more than two adjacent sections that serve the same rhetorical function.

## Gate

Check that removing any section would create a visible reasoning gap. Ensure every material factual section has evidence and the conclusion does not introduce a new major premise. If evidence cannot support the intended thesis, narrow the thesis before drafting.
